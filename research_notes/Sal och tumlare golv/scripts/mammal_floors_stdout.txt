5. Porpoise biosonar bed dead zone: a target closer to the bed than c tau / 2 merges
   with the bed echo (normal incidence). The beam footprint adds R (1 - cos(half-beam)):
   porpoise -3 dB beam ~16 deg [S, Miller & Wahlberg 2013], half-beam 8 deg.
   click duration  44 us ->  3.3 cm
   click duration  77 us ->  5.8 cm
   click duration 113 us ->  8.5 cm
   click duration 175 us -> 13.1 cm
   beam term at range 1 m: + 1.0 cm
   beam term at range 2 m: + 1.9 cm
   beam term at range 5 m: + 4.9 cm
   Dead zone: a few cm close in, up to ~1-2 dm at 5 m.
   A cod, haddock or whiting lying on the bed is 5-15 cm high and carries a swimbladder
   (90-95 % of the backscatter, E13): above the dead zone in the final approach (< 2 m),
   at its edge from ~5 m. A flatfish has no swimbladder and, buried, lies inside it.
