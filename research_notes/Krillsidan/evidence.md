## K0. Why the krill pairs matter

- Krill is 0.84 of whiting's uncapped potential (fill_decomp_all, fix105) and most of the planktivores' outside
  spring (potential_season: zooplankton X 0.05 in Jan vs 12 in May; krill carries Jul-Mar).
- "Mötesfaktorn per grupp" (E2): winter stomach anchors give a krill factor ~0.01 (whiting 0.0099, planktivores
  0.012) at the library density D_lib. On copy a (krill x0.012, zooplankton x0.36, fish x0.16) the planktivores'
  krill fell to 0.03 of the potential and the diet gate put planktivores -> krill at implied M2 0.10 vs booked
  2.07 (OFF).
- Three readings were open: the winter factor does not hold all year; D at the sites is far below 5 t/km2; or
  the booked krill diet share (planktivores 0.398, whiting 0.56 of the booked flow) is wrong.

## K3. Measured krill density at a stomach site

- No co-located pair (stomach ration R and krill density D, same place and time) exists for any model species in
  the notes or in two narrow searches (Oslofjord/fjord krill + fish diet; Barents juvenile cod / krill
  availability). Onsrud 2004 and Onsrud, Kaartvedt & Breien 2005 (held in full) report acoustics of fish and krill
  layers but no krill density [F, checked: no abundance figure in either text].
- Nearest same-place, same-season pair [J]: in-box winter-night IKMT 1987-91 (Bøhle & Moksness, "Krill i
  Skagerrak"): 1.7-4.8 t/km2 in 5-65 m, column 7-9 t/km2 after the catch efficiency 0.6 [J], floor ~3 [D];
  against the Skagerrak and NE North Sea Q1 stomachs of 1980-81 (other years). At D 7-9 the winter factors are
  x0.55-0.7 of their D_lib 5 values; at the floor 3, x1.7.
- PROXY with D measured but no usable ration: Wilson, Jump & Buchheister 2009 (juvenile walleye pollock, Gulf of
  Alaska, September; area means 1.2-13.7 g/m2 [F/D]): per-capita krill numbers rise with area krill density
  (rho 0.41-0.51), but the ration is one fixed value per group, so no a can be computed [F, held in "Aptit"].
  Kock 1994 (icefish) gives between-year content without station densities.
- Others named but not obtained: Dalpadado & Bogstad 2004 (Barents juvenile cod diet vs krill; search summary
  only), Orlova et al. 2005 (Barents cod vs bottom-layer krill; fragments).

## K4. The factor the booked flows imply, pair by pair (scripts/booked_level.py) [D]

Multi-prey Type II with one handling budget, T = 1 (the factor then contains any non-foraging time), equal cells,
budget_gate's visible shares and the krill pairs' depth table x the pool shape:
R_j = M2_j B_j / B_g / 1460, S = sum_j h_j R_j, f_j = R_j / ((1 - S) a_j B_vis,j).

| predator | S (booked) | chain X | common f | krill f_j | other f_j |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| planktivores | 0.205 | 9.9 | 0.026 | 0.029 | zooplankton 0.024 |
| whiting | 0.409 | 24.9 | 0.028 | 0.022 | planktivores 0.030, epifauna 0.059, benthivores 0.10, zoo 0.073 |
| water column | 0.346 | 17.1 | 0.031 | 0.029 | planktivores 0.034, whiting 0.024, benthivores 0.015 |
| mackerel | 0.093 | 2.1 | 0.049 | 0.032 | zooplankton 0.095 (Macy lab clearance), planktivores 0.036 |
| benthivores | 0.504 | 142 | 0.0072 | 0.0087 | infauna 0.0070, epifauna 0.0072 |

- On the booked flows the krill pairs are NOT out of line: within each predator the krill f_j sits with the
  others (0.022-0.032 vs 0.015-0.10). The live diet gate passes for the same reason (shares).
- Against the stomach anchors of E2: whiting 0.0099 (gm) vs 0.022, planktivores 0.012 vs 0.029. The x2-2.5 is
  conventions, not a conflict [D]: the anchors place the fish on 100-150 m beds (whiting a_eff x0.75 of a vs x0.55
  at the equal-cell krill-weighted mean), use 1/(1 - h R_krill) instead of the shared 1/(1 - S), and for the
  planktivores take the winter ration (group Nov-Mar 0.24 %BW/d vs the booked annual 0.60).
- What breaks copy a is the MIX of anchor types: the clearance / slope anchors (zooplankton V1 0.36, Kulke sprat
  0.6-1.2, Rindorf fish slope 0.16-0.30, mackerel's Macy clearance at x0.095/0.032 = 3x the krill) sit 5-15x
  above the booked-flow factors, while the stomach-ration-at-density anchors (krill) sit on them. The split is
  by METHOD, not by prey type [D/J].
- Pout check [D/J]: the booked pout krill ration is ~0.8 %BW/d annual (0.66 x Q/B 4.5) before 268's rescale to
  the group Q/B 6.2; measured pout krill in winter (when Albert's content is highest) is 0.20 (NE North Sea 1980)
  to 0.75 (Oslofjord March). The booked pout krill flow is at or above the top of what the stomachs give; the
  planktivores' booked krill share may be high by up to x1.5-4 [J].

| mackerel | 0.65 / 0.61 / 1.54 / 1.20 | 1981: 2.01 / 0.46 / 0.48 / 1.05 | 2.45 / 0.61 / 0.25 / 0.70 | 9.8 (1991: 346) |

- Every series puts the factor LOW in Q3 (0.12-0.57) and high in Q1-Q2: the chain's krill encounter peaks in Q3-Q4
  (summer ingestion q 1.30 for planktivores and mackerel; krill season 1.1-1.3; Q2 0.6), the field's krill intake
  in Q1-Q2.
- A constant factor would therefore put x3-5 too much krill in Q3 relative to Q1-Q2 (pout, Oslofjord whiting, 1981
  saithe and mackerel), more on the 1991 CRR shares.
- Candidate physical drivers [J], none carried as such by the model:
  1. Availability in the vertical: overwintering krill in a dense, deep day layer that the gadoids sit in, against
     a summer krill that migrates further and spreads (the model's krill depth shape is fixed over the year).
  2. Krill size: adults largest in winter-spring, the new cohort small in autumn (E 5000 MJ/t flat in the pool; "Krill
     i Skagerrak" gives 4.3 kJ/g in Q2-Q3, 6 in Q4-Q1).
  3. The library's krill season (Q2 0.6, Q4 1.3) rests on "winter IKMT catches high, spring trawl catches low"; the
     Q2 stomach pulse (saithe 1991, whiting April 1991) argues that Q2 krill was available to fish.
  4. The fish's summer switch to other prey (sandeel, 0-group fish, Calanus); shared handling carries a switch only
     as far as the model's other prey rise in summer.
  5. The ingestion Q10 multiplies the krill a in summer (q 1.3); the field Q3 krill minimum runs against it.
- The seasonal reading rests on krill SHARES (whiting: rations) of mixed years and regions; the whiting Q3 value is
  a judgement from near-zero North Sea and thin 2022 Skagerrak samples.
