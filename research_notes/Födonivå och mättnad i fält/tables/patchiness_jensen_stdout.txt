Caveat (J): this Jensen penalty assumes saturation acts on the patch scale. The engine's
1/h is a gastric-evacuation (digestion) ceiling with a gut time of ~1-2 days; a fish that
crosses many sub-km patches per gut time integrates the encounters linearly, so the
penalty applies only if fish stay within one patch for longer than the gut time.
