3. Bull 1996 thesis ch. 3.4 (salmon parr, excess food 24 h, ambient T,
   simulated natural photoperiod): lipid trajectory of controls (READ)
========================================================================
  lipid 5.32 % BW (Sep) -> 2.63 % BW (Apr): ratio 0.49, loss 2.69 % BW over ~213 d = 1.26 % BW per 100 d
  Deprivation losses (3 wk each): early 1.12, mid 1.00, late 1.94 % BW;
  appetite rose above controls only after the EARLY (Oct) deprivation, for
  14 d, then fell back to control level; fat gain during refeeding +0.49 % BW
  (early), -0.05 (mid, Dec), -0.37 (late, Feb): only the early deficit was
  partly restored (fig. 3.12, 3.14).
  ch. 3.5 (photoperiod, ad lib 24 h, same ambient T): after the Nov deficit
  restoration +1.02 (acc.), +1.27 (del.), +1.91 (control) % BW in 3 wk;
  after the Jan deficit the group perceiving April did NOT restore,
  the group perceiving October and the controls did (in this second year the
  controls restored a Jan deficit; in ch. 3.4 a Dec deficit was not restored).
