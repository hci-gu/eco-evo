Q2Q3_notes.txt - search-side and handling-side mechanisms for benthic pairs
==========================================================================
Fork of the research subagent, 2026-10-27. Research only. Evidence rows with sources:
tables/Q2Q3_evidence.csv. Scripts: scripts/Q3_item_handling.py, scripts/Q3_ices_items.py,
scripts/Q2Q3_openalex.py (search helper). Tags: [F] full text read, [A] abstract only,
[S] secondary (citing source named), [D] data computed here, J judgement.
Not repeated here (already in "Exponering av bottenbyten"/tables/B_behaviour_evidence.txt and
"Attackrater ur motesteori"/benthivores_encounter.md): search speeds, contact widths, Brawn 1969,
Persson 2012 attack rates, de Groot 1969, Hurst 2007 light thresholds, Holmes 1979 (internal only).

0. Headline
-----------
1. No search-side factor of 5-50x was found. Telemetry puts cod on or near the bed 57-90 % of
   the time [F]; a time-budget share lowers realised a by at most ~1-3x and the stomach buffers
   it (previous note, gut_buffer_window.py). The one large factor on the search side is prey
   burial (Persson 2012: burying Crangon 12-32x below exposed prey [F]); it is already inside e.
2. Encounter theory says the group sweeps the whole bed 1.6 times a day (a_enc 0.26 x 1.53 t/km2
   x 4 ticks; with e: infauna 0.22 /d, epifauna 0.92 /d) [J]. At that sweep the exposed parts of
   the infauna would be cropped as fast as they appear. The field record on cropped parts is of
   this kind: flatfish consumption of lugworm tail tips about equals the loss/regeneration of
   tail tips (de Vlas 1979 via Lees 2024 [S]); 13-16 % of Amphiura production goes to
   regeneration, 25-58 % of body weight is regenerated tissue [S]. That is supply (renewal)
   limitation, not encounter limitation - it points at the refuge/exchange term, not at a.
3. Handling: for whole items (polychaetes, small bivalves, crustaceans, Nephrops) per-item
   handling is <= 3 h/d at Cmax and h_handle/h_digest <= 0.2: digestion-limited, h = E/Cmax holds
   [D/J]. For CROPPED PARTS (arm tips ~2 mg, siphon tips ~1-10 mg) a fish at Cmax needs 7 000-
   40 000 items/d: 6-34 h/d of handling at 3 s/item; h_handle/h_digest 0.23-1.4 (0.5 kg - 2 kg
   fish). By Jeschke 2002 (y_max = 1/max(b, c)) a large fish cannot reach Cmax on cropped parts
   alone; handling only runs while foraging, so a 12 h window halves that side again [J].
   Direction: this LOWERS the ceiling on parts and RAISES x; it does not unsaturate the pair.
4. Armour: crustacean evacuation ~2x slower than fish/soft prey of equal energy density
   (Andersen 1999 whiting/Crangon [A]); crabs add a 0-33 h shell-cracking lag and halve the rate
   (Andersen, Chabot & Couturier 2016 [A]); satiation meal of 1-group cod on crabs 2.8 % WW vs
   Crangon 9.1 % vs plaice 17.8 % (Arnott & Pihl 2000 [F]). Factor on h: shrimps x1.7-2,
   crabs/hermits x2-3, Nephrops ~x2 (J; no direct Nephrops measurement).

1. Q2(a) time budgets from telemetry
------------------------------------
- Cod, southern North Sea (Hobson et al. 2007 [F]): "cod were closely associated with the seabed
  for 90% of the time"; seabed behaviour (SB) 70 % of each month, seabed-with-ascents 20.5 %.
- Cod, North Sea/Channel (Hobson et al. 2009 [F]): near seabed 57 %, midwater 43 % of time;
  residence (< 20 km from a centre) 67 % of time, directed movement 17.6 +- 11.4 km/d.
- Coastal Skagerrak cod (Espeland et al. 2007 [A]): home range 27 ha in the spawning season.
  An individual sweeping 250-1300 m2/d (22-50 cm, encounter v x w, 24 h, J) covers 27 ha in
  200-1100 days alone; local depletion at the home-range scale is then set by fish density, not
  by the individual (J).
- Plaice (Hunter et al. 2004 [F]): vertical (off-bottom) activity mainly at night and in the
  migration season; no on-bottom foraging share reported. Burrows 1994 (2-5 % in motion in light
  with excess food) is satiation, not a time limit (previous note).
- No accelerometry-based foraging time budget for wild cod, haddock, plaice or dab was found
  (sea bass and mesocosm cod only: MEPS 496:19 sea bass [A]; JEB 2016 219:695 mesocosm cod scouring
  [A]). Gap.
- Reading (J): the foraging-time share for the benthivores is 0.5-0.9 (cod, haddock), 0.3-0.6
  (flatfish); realised a / encounter a from time budget alone 0.3-0.9, i.e. <= 3x.

2. Q2(b) detection - nothing new beyond the previous notes (contact sense for gadoids, chemo/
   visual for flatfish, "Food covered by sand was not found", Brawn 1969 [A]).

3. Q2(c) strike success and yield on retracting prey
----------------------------------------------------
- No laboratory strike-success figure on retracting siphons, Amphiura arms or polychaete heads
  was found (gap). Holmes 1979 capture 99-100 % on free worms (internal only) is an upper bound.
- Yield per attack: "flatfish removed only one or a few tail segments per attack" (de Vlas 1979b
  via Lees 2024 [S]); 2.2-3.8 nips per lugworm per month on the Balgzand.
- Renewal: Amphiura regenerated tissue ~25 % (up to 50 %) of body weight (Bowmer & Keegan 1983
  [S]), 57.9 % lost over 21 months in A. chiajei (Munday 1993 [S]); regeneration 13 % of
  A. filiformis production in the Skagerrak (Skold et al., via Lees [S]); siphon regeneration
  ~0.3 mg dry/week/individual in Tellina (Trevallion 1971 [S]).
- Reading (J): on cropped parts the intake is bounded by renewal (the infauna refuge exchange
  k ~2.5 /yr in "Refugium och utbyte i bentos"), consistent with fish eating 1.3-6.5 % of
  infauna production. This bounds intake on parts; it does not lower a.

4. Q2(d) giving-up / patch leaving
----------------------------------
- Juvenile plaice switch to area-restricted search after attacks when prey are aggregated and to
  win-shift when dispersed; intensive search triggered by local density (Hill et al. 2002 [A]);
  inconspicuous prey are detected in pauses (saltatory search, Hill et al. 2003 [A]).
- No giving-up density or patch residence time for adult gadoids or flatfish on benthos (gap).
  ARS means realised encounter tracks LOCAL density: fish concentrate where prey are dense and
  leave depleted patches - the opposite of a uniform-mixing assumption, and a reason intake per
  fish responds to prey per fish (J).

5. Q2(e) are exposed epifauna met at encounter rates?
-----------------------------------------------------
- Lab: cod a on burying Crangon 1.8 vs exposed mysids 57 and cryptic Palaemon 21 cm2/s (Persson
  2012 [F], via previous note) - in e already (small shrimps 0.35).
- Field: Arnott & Pihl 2000 [F]: species selection by 1-group cod in Gullmarsvik was not explained
  by handling profitability, satiation level or evacuation; the authors name evasiveness,
  abundance, patchiness and light. No offshore tethering or in situ predation rate per fish on
  crabs, hermits, Munida, Pandalus or Nephrops was found (gap).
- Diel exposure of Pandalus (0.75 on bottom) and small shrimps (Ljungberg 2012) is in the
  refuge and exposure notes; not repeated.
- Flag for Q1 (outside this sub-task): Freitas et al. 2012 MEPS 471:203 [A]: 0-group plaice
  realised growth below the DEB maximum, declining through the season, without benthic
  biomass gradients; authors infer competition.

6. Q3 handling per item (scripts/Q3_item_handling.py; tables/Q3_item_handling_stdout.txt)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Inputs: ceiling 104.7 kJ/kg/d; E 2.6 / 3.75 / 3.6 kJ/g (library); item masses from the ICES
stomach database (box 51-61 N, 4 W-13 E; tables/Q3_ices_items_stdout.txt [D]) and Duineveld &
van Noort 1986 [A]; handling times from Arnott & Pihl 2000 [F] (crabs 9 s, 5-14; Crangon
ln HT = -1.73 + 21.4 P:C + 0.78 SF -> 4-37 s), Persson 2012 (~1 s mysids, J), J for parts.
Central results (0.5 kg / 2 kg fish):
  arm tips 2 mg, 3 s:      10 000 / 40 000 items/d, 8.4 / 33.6 h/d, h_handle/h_digest 0.35 / 1.40
  siphon tips 3 mg, 3 s:    6 700 / 27 000 items/d, 5.6 / 22.4 h/d, ratio 0.23 / 0.93
  polychaetes 30 mg, 4 s:     670 /  2 700, 0.7 / 3.0 h/d, ratio 0.03 / 0.12
  amphipods 10 mg, 2 s:     2 000 /  8 000, 1.1 / 4.5 h/d, ratio 0.05 / 0.19
  crustacean epifauna and Nephrops: <= 0.6 h/d, ratio <= 0.03
Ranges (mass and time low-high) span ~0.5-56 h/d for parts at 0.5 kg. Field stomachs hold
1.1-1.5 benthic items per non-empty stomach on average (p90 3-4) [D] - a snapshot, consistent
with few, larger items in gadoids; plaice items are small (polychaete 0.03 g, ophiuroid 0.016 g).
Reading (J): h = E/Cmax holds for every whole-item prey class; on cropped parts a larger
fish is handling-limited (SSS), so cropped parts cannot carry a large fish's ration - this caps
the infauna PART of the diet, raising h on that part by up to ~1.4x (2 kg fish) and more if
foraging is restricted to part of the day.

7. Q3 armour
------------
- Whiting: Crangon "took almost twice as long" to evacuate as fish of equal energy density; krill
  like fish (Andersen 1999 [A]). Energy density 3.4 -> 5.6 kJ/g alone raises evacuation time
  x1.6-1.7 [A].
- Cod/whiting: a shell-cracking stage of 0-33 h and both stage rates halved with armour (krill <
  shrimps < crabs) (Andersen, Chabot & Couturier 2016 [A]; full text not obtained - Wiley 403).
- Cod model (Fall & Fiksen 2019 [F], Andersen/Temming parameters): a full stomach of one prey
  type takes 3 d (amphipods) to 11 d (Pandalus) at 1.5 C; profitability = min(handling, digestive
  quality) per Jeschke 2002.
- Satiation meal (Arnott & Pihl 2000 [F]): crabs 2.8 % WW (0.33 % AFDW) vs Crangon 9.1 % (2.13 %)
  vs plaice 17.8 % of cod wet weight.
- Factor on h relative to soft prey at equal energy density (J): shrimps x1.7-2 (Andersen 1999),
  crabs/hermits x2-3 (2016 halving + lag; meal-size result), mysids/krill ~x1, Nephrops ~x2
  (no measurement), ophiuroids/bivalves: ash and carbonate slow evacuation (Couturier et al.
  2013 [A], previous note) - unquantified.
