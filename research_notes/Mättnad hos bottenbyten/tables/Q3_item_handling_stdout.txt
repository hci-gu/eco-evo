ratio = h_handle / h_digest. Jeschke 2002 SSS: the ceiling is 1/max(b, c); handling binds where
ratio > 1 with 24 h foraging, or > 0.5 with 12 h foraging (handling only runs while foraging).

Where handling would bind (central masses and times):
  Amphiura arm tip (cropped) at 2.0 kg: 33.6 h/d of handling > 24 h foraging
  Amphiura arm tip (cropped) at 2.0 kg: 33.6 h/d of handling > 12 h foraging
  Bivalve siphon tip (cropped) at 2.0 kg: 22.4 h/d of handling > 12 h foraging

Direction (J): a larger h (handling-limited) LOWERS the ceiling on that item class but RAISES
x = a h f B; it does not unsaturate the pair. It matters for which items can supply a
large fish at Cmax: cropped parts (arm tips, siphon tips) cannot.
