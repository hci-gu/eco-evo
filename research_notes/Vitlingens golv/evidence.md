# Whiting's hide floor - evidence (2026-11-11)

Grades: [F] field, [L] lab, [A] abstract or search snippet only, [S] secondary (cited in another note
or paper), [D] derived here, [J] judgement. Downloads: only Unpaywall records were returned (no open
PDF for any DOI tried; records in `papers/`). E1 and E4 were read as page scans served by VLIZ

## Sources

- **E1** Dahl, E. 1961. The association between young whiting, Gadus merlangus, and the jelly-fish
  Cyanea capillata. Sarsia 3: 47-55. [L/F, full text read]
  - Raunefjord (Bergen), July 1960; aquarium work with "a dozen whiting measuring between 3 and 9 cm";
    small whiting 4-6 cm.
  - "In the aquaria the whiting generally did not show any close association with Cyanea. For long
    periods they moved about quite independently of them but now and then a fish would post itself
    among the dangling tentacles" (p. 49).
  - Conclusion 1: young whiting seek Cyanea "mainly because of the protection offered. When frightened
    by objects moving above or in the water they are likely to hide among the tentacles but sometimes
    also dive towards the depths away from the jelly-fish" (p. 54).
  - Whiting caught among Cyanea near the surface had fed on plankton (7 of 7: calanoid copepods,
    especially Calanus finmarchicus, decapod larvae, chaetognaths); no jellyfish tissue, no Hyperia
    (p. 52, 54). Feeding is not suspended while associated.
  - Contact with tentacles rarely harmful to whiting (few, single nematocysts on the skin) vs fatal to
    Gobius flavescens (100-300 per mm2) (p. 51).
  - No predator was present in any trial; the protection is inferred, not measured.
- **E2** Lynam, C.P. & Brierley, A.S. 2007. Enhanced survival of 0-group gadoid fish under jellyfish
  umbrellas. Mar. Biol. 150: 1397-1401. doi:10.1007/s00227-006-0429-7 [A]
  - ICES 0-group gadoid surveys 1971-1986 (June-July pelagic trawls); whiting residual survival (1-group
    recruits vs a Ricker expectation) correlates with Cyanea lamarckii, C. capillata and combined
    abundance, R >= 0.60, P < 0.01, N = 15. Cod, haddock, Norway pout also tested; whiting is the
    species named in the conclusion. Mechanism proposed (refuge among tentacles), not tested.
    Correlation at stock scale over 15 years.
- **E3** Hay, S.J., Hislop, J.R.G. & Shanks, A.M. 1990. North Sea Scyphomedusae; summer distribution,
  estimated biomass and significance particularly for 0-group gadoid fish. Neth. J. Sea Res. 25:
  113-130. doi:10.1016/0077-7579(90)90013-7 [A]
  - June-July 0-group gadoid survey by-catch 1971-1986 (no 1984); Aurelia, C. capillata, C. lamarckii;
    the data were "examined for evidence" of shelter relationships with whiting and haddock. The
    outcome of that test was not retrievable (no open copy; publisher 403). Large inter-annual and
    regional variation in medusa abundance.
- **E4** Lynam, C.P., Hay, S.J. & Brierley, A.S. 2004. Interannual variability in abundance of North
  Sea jellyfish and links to the North Atlantic Oscillation. Limnol. Oceanogr. 49: 637-643. [F, pp.
  637-638 read]
  - The E2/E3 medusa data: IYGPT pelagic trawl, June-July (August in 1971-72), 2030 trawls, > 430 000
    medusae (1-47 cm); one-hour stepped haul (near the bed, midwater, 5-10 m). One of the four areas
    is "west of northern Denmark" (adjacent to the Skagerrak). Medusa catches heavily skewed (many nil
    catches, a few very high): patchy.
- **E5** Eriksen, E. et al. 2012. Biomass of scyphozoan jellyfish, and its spatial association with
  0-group fish in the Barents Sea. PLoS ONE 7: e33050 (PMC3310840). [F/A]
  - Cites shelter of young gadoids (cod, haddock, pollock, saithe, whiting) among tentacles "to avoid
    predation" (Mansueti 1963; Lynam & Brierley 2007); no test. GAM spatial associations of medusa
    biomass with 0-group fish modest and area-dependent (haddock, herring, cod, capelin; adj. R2
    0.24-0.55).
- **E6** Atkinson, C.J.L., Bergmann, M. & Kaiser, M.J. 2004. Habitat selection in whiting. J. Fish
  Biol. 64: 788-793. doi:10.1111/j.1095-8649.2004.00340.x [A, L]
  - Tank choice (60 x 60 x 90 cm tanks, rod-caught fish): whiting spent most time over sand, then
    gravel, least over a habitat with emergent structures. A predator stimulus (a shadow passed over
    the tank) increased large whiting's preference for SAND and small whiting's for the EMERGENT
    STRUCTURE habitat. Sizes, time shares and feeding not available (abstract only).
- **E7** Bastrikin, D.K., Gallego, A., Millar, C.P., Priede, I.G. & Jones, E.G. 2014. Settlement length
  and temporal settlement patterns of juvenile cod, haddock and whiting in a northern North Sea
  coastal nursery area. ICES J. Mar. Sci. 71: 2101-. doi:10.1093/icesjms/fsu029 [A]
  - Whiting settle at ~85 +- 6 mm, from early June to early August (later and longer than cod 49 mm,
    haddock 78 mm).
- **E8** Bromley, P.J. & Kell, L.T. 1999. Vertical migration and spatial distribution of pelagic
  0-group gadoids prior to and during settlement in the North Sea. Acta Adriatica 40(2): 7-17. [A]
  - June, northern North Sea: pelagic 0-group (~3 cm) in diel vertical migration, timing variable;
    near-surface concentration interpreted as feeding on migrating copepods. Cod 4-6 cm demersal by
    day, pelagic at night during settlement (July); > 8 cm fully demersal.
- **E9** Bromley, P.J., Watson, T. & Hislop, J.R.G. 1997. Diel feeding patterns and the development of
  food webs in pelagic 0-group cod, haddock, whiting, saithe and Norway pout in the northern North
  Sea. ICES J. Mar. Sci. 54: 846-853. [A, snippet]
  - Diel feeding size- and species-specific; whiting "subject to substantial levels of cannibalism and
    inter-specific predation" in the pelagic 0-group phase.
- **E10** Walker-Milne, N., Elliott, S.A.M., Wright, P.J. & Bailey, D.M. 2025. Seascape ecology of
  juvenile gadoid nursery areas. R. Soc. Open Sci. 12: 250705. doi:10.1098/rsos.250705 [A/S, abstract
  + GWCT summary]
  - Baited stereo video, Firth of Clyde, 488 deployments, juveniles 20-120 mm: whiting in deeper water
    over smoother terrain, most likely "just outside the edges of algal patches, not inside them"
    (cod in shallow algae-rich areas). Juvenile whiting do not occupy structure in the field.
- **E11** Pedersen, J. 2000 (J. Fish Biol.), via Temming et al. 2004 and a search summary [S]: pelagic
  North Sea whiting fed at night, demersal whiting by day. Mergardt & Temming 1997 (ICES J. Mar. Sci.
  54: 226-242) [A]: whiting on sandeel, one feeding peak 22-24 h, minimum 08-10 h. Temming et al.
  2004 (J. Fish Biol. 64: 1351-1372) [A]: whiting and haddock stomach contents rise rapidly at night
  (sandeel burying). Rindorf 2003 (thesis) [S]: whiting take benthic prey at night, clupeids and
  gadoids at dawn and dusk.
  - Whiting feed by day and by night depending on prey and position; no daily inactive period.
- **E12** Petrakis, G., MacLennan, D.N. & Newton, A.W. 2001. Day-night and depth effects on catch rates
  during trawl surveys in the North Sea. ICES J. Mar. Sci. 58: 50-60. doi:10.1006/jmsc.2000.0989 [S,
  as cited in research_notes/"Overlapp i fiskparen"]: no adult whiting day/night catch difference.
- **E13** Onsrud, M.S.R., Kaartvedt, S., Rostad, A. & Klevjer, T.A. 2004. ICES J. Mar. Sci. (doi:
  10.1016/j.icesjms.2004.09.005) [S, via "Overlapp i fiskparen"]: whiting 20-45 m above a 120 m bed
  (Oslofjord); no diel difference in their krill feeding (stomachs).

## Model-side facts (repository, read-only; `scripts/floor_mechanics_stdout.txt`)

- **M1** `whiting.visibility_floor` 0.5, comment "Judgement: whiting on the bed hide better than
  midwater schools" (fg_library.yaml).
- **M2** Checked pairs on whiting: benthivores (M2 0.18, a 0.0226, stage_access [0.68, 0.24],
  dark_ratio 0.168) and water column (M2 0.157, a 0.0359, [0.41, 0.08], 0.137) carry NO pair floor,
  so the species floor 0.5 applies to both; seals (0.85) and porpoises (0.75) have pair floors (out of
  scope here).
- **M3** Observers of whiting (`*_observes_whiting: true`): benthivores, water column, mackerel,
  planktivores (whiting eats them), porpoises, seals, whiting itself (own biomass never attenuated).
  The species floor sets what benthivores, the water column, mackerel and planktivores see.
- **M4** The engine has one hidden share per DM cell and one floor per (observer, prey) pair, applied
  to the whole cell biomass before stage_access; J and A hide alike.
- **M5** Whiting as prey in the band derivations: 0.7 day / 0.5 night in the bottom 10 m, the rest
  10-40 m (section 251; research_notes/"Bottenfiskens hojdprofil"); predators: cod 0.70 in the bottom
  2 m by day (benthivores), saithe exp 15 m (water column). Rule A's light is on every pair.
- **M6** Every fish-pair a assumes the prey cruising at u = 0.10 m/s (Gerritsen-Strickler v_rel;
  `pred_encounter_a.py` u_smallfish, `benth_encounter.py` U_PREY); predator search speeds saithe
  1.0 BL/s (night x0.6), cod 0.4 BL/s.
- **M7** Rest pays 1.0 x resting_metabolism (3.87 MJ/t/tick, SMR at the bottom layer), i.e. the
  engine's rest is a state at standard metabolism, without swimming.
- **M8** fix97 annual-mean rest share of whiting 0.066 (research_notes/"Gom och sinnen" section 4).
- **M9** Combined derivation (research_notes/"Fangst och stim i fiskparen"): whiting as prey sigma 0.1
  day / 0.05 night, F 0.985-0.988; capture p 0.2 -> 0.59-0.62 with q 0.8. Today's p 0.2 is a lab
  per-strike success on single prey (Turesson & Bronmark 2004), i.e. it already contains the prey's
  evasion after the strike.

## Derived (`scripts/floor_mechanics.py`)

- **D1** Cut on predation and observation = rest x (1 - floor): at rest 0.066, floor 0.5 -> 0.033;
  0.85 -> 0.0099; 1.0 -> 0.
- **D2** Box GOV whiting length table (q-corrected): biomass share <= 9 cm (Dahl's shelter sizes,
  below settlement at 8.5 cm) is 0.027 of B in Q3 2001-25 (0.043 in 1991-96), ~0 in Q1, Q2, Q4.
  Cell-level floor the jellyfish shelter could at most justify (all small fish associated, perfect
  shelter): 0.973 in Jun-Aug (0.957 for 1991-96), annual equivalent 0.993; at half associated and
  residual exposure 0.5: 0.993 (annual 0.998). Caveat: the table is a bottom-trawl catch and does not
  see the pre-settlement pelagic 0-group near the surface; 0.027 is the share of the model's
  (GOV-based) whiting biomass that could shelter, not the field share of sheltering fish.
- **D3** Stationary-prey encounter factor v_rel(v, 0) / v_rel(v, 0.10): saithe 0.984-0.993 by day,
  0.956 at night speed (0.59 only for an implausibly slow 0.07 m/s searcher); cod 0.81-0.92 at
  0.4 BL/s and 30-50 cm (0.66 at 0.2 BL/s).
