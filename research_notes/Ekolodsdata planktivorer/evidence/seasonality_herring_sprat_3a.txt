Seasonality of herring and sprat in the Skagerrak / Kattegat (Div. 3.a) - evidence
==================================================================================
Compiled 2026-10-24.

1. WBSS migration cycle - ICES stock annex [F]
 ICES 2026. Stock Annex: Herring (Clupea harengus) in Skagerrak, Kattegat, western Baltic (subdivision
 20-24), and the eastern part of divisions 4.a and 4.b (her.27.20-24). August 2026. figshare 33296379,
 https://ndownloader.figshare.com/files/67698360 . Section 1.1, PDF p. 6:
 "The majority of 2+ ringers migrate out of the area during the 2nd quarter of the year, through the
 Sound and Belt Sea and propagate into the western part of the Skagerrak and the eastern North Sea to
 feed (Payne et al., 2009). The extent of the migration is age dependent, where the younger individuals
 migrate up into Kattegat and Skagerrak and the older fish migrate all the way out into the eastern North
 Sea. Towards the end of summer, herring aggregates in the eastern Skagerrak and Kattegat before they
 migrate to the main wintering areas in the southern part of the Kattegat, the Sound and the western
 Baltic (ICES, 1991; Nielsen et al., 2001). The extent of migration is season-dependent and variable over
 time (Clausen et al., 2006)."
 "... for the largest herring stock (the Rugen herring) an outmigration from the spawning sites during
 April-June through all Belts. This migration is not performed in large dense schools; these form during
 the summer feeding in Skagerrak and Kattegat. The school formation is retained during the
 overwintering, which mainly occurs in the Southern Kattegat and the Sound."
 Section 1.1: "Most populations are migratory and frequently mix on shared feeding and wintering grounds";
 populations include WBSS-Rugen, WBSS-W. Baltic, Inner Danish Waters, "from the Skagerrak (WBSS-SK)",
 and NSAS and central Baltic herring "co-occur in the WBSS distribution area throughout the year".
 Section 2.3.2 (PDF p. 16): HERAS "has very limited coverage of the youngest WBSS age groups (1-2
 ringers)". Section 2.3.3 (PDF p. 17): IBTS in 3.a is run in Q1 (February) and Q3 (August-September);
 "The IBTS quarter 1 and quarter 3 coverage is limited to SD 20-21 (the Skagerrak and the Kattegat)."
 Also used: GERAS (October, western Baltic) ages 1-3; HERAS ages 2-6+.
 Older version: ICES 2023 stock annex her.27.20-24 (figshare 23898678) - same narrative.

2. NSAS juveniles in 3.a [F]
 ICES 2022. Stock Annex her.27.3a47d (NSAS), figshare 18622589: "Juveniles are caught in Division 3.a
 and as bycatch in the industrial fishery" (Fishery section, PDF p. 1; NSAS juveniles in 3.a are taken in the Danish/Swedish fleets C and D).
 HERAS 2023 (p. 8): "Juvenile herring were primarily in the usual distribution in the shallower south and
 eastern parts of the North Sea and in the Skagerrak and Kattegat".
 Dana 2019 report Table 9: stratum 31 NSAS biomass 45.8 kt of which 41.6 kt 1-ringers (mean 46 g);
 maturity of Skagerrak NSAS: 99.7 % of 1-ringers and 87.8 % of 2-ringers immature.
 1989 Argos survey (ICES CM 1990/H:24): "all herring of 2 group and older could be assigned to local
 spring spawner stock. North Sea autumn spawners dominated the 0- and 1-group."
 -> in summer the eastern Skagerrak holds mainly juvenile NSAS (0-2 ringers) plus WBSS of mixed ages.

5. Sprat
 HERAS 2023 p. 10: "In some years (2013 and 2018), sprat has exclusively been found in the Kattegat."
 HERAS 2016-2025 (July): sprat in stratum 31 = 0 t in 7 of 9 years (134 t 2021, 1 685 t 2023); in 41 = 0
 every year; 42 = 0-1 791 t. 3.a sprat sits in the Kattegat (stratum 21) in summer.
 1989 Argos (early August): sprat in Skagerrak strata 2 t total vs 2 085 t in the Kattegat.
 ICES 2019 Stock annex spr.27.3a4 (figshare 18623360): sprat in Kattegat and Skagerrak "appear to be a
 mixture of genetically North Sea sprat and genetical hybrids near the Swedish coast"; "Sprat in the
 North Sea has a prolonged spawning season ranging from early spring to late autumn".
 Danish industrial sprat fishery in 3.a is mainly in autumn/winter (not quantified here - gap).

6. Fraction of the year's herring in the eastern Skagerrak, summer vs winter: NOT FOUND as a number.
 Qualitative consensus (stock annex): summer feeding aggregation in the Skagerrak/Kattegat and eastern
 North Sea (Q2-Q3), a pre-wintering aggregation in the eastern Skagerrak/Kattegat at the end of summer,
 and overwintering mainly in the southern Kattegat, the Sound and the western Baltic (Q4-Q1); juvenile
 NSAS use 3.a as nursery year-round. Ulrich et al. 2012 (DTU conference paper, "Modelling the mixing of
 herring stocks between the Baltic and the North Sea from otolith data", orbit.dtu.dk) model seasonal
 NSAS/WBSS composition by GLMM and report "a clear seasonal and age-related pattern" [A] - full text
 (J0212.pdf) not read. Staehr & Worsoe Clausen 2012 (DTU conference abstract, "High-resolution acoustic
 indices of Atlantic herring ... ICES Divisions IIIa and SD 22-24") used Dana HERAS 2006-2011
 abundance by ICES rectangle split by stock [A] - rectangle maps not retrieved.
 Nielsen et al. (2001) and ICES (1991) are the stock annex's sources for the end-of-summer aggregation
 in the eastern Skagerrak/Kattegat and the wintering areas [S; not read, and the annex's reference list
 entry was not extracted].
