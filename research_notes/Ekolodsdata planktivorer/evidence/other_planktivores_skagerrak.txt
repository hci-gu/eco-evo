Other planktivores in the Skagerrak / Norwegian Trench - biomass levels found
============================================================================
Compiled 2026-10-24. Time-boxed search; several gaps. Tags: [F] full text, [A] abstract/snippet, [S] secondary.

1. Blue whiting, Norway pout, mackerel, horse mackerel in an acoustic survey of the eastern Skagerrak [F]
 Kirkegaard et al. 1990, ICES C.M. 1990/H:24 (Argos section, Hagstrom & Palmen), Table 28, PDF p. 27.
 https://www.ices.dk/sites/pub/CM%20Doccuments/1990/H/1990_H24.pdf
 Early August 1989, Skagerrak strata C3-C5, D2-D5 (east of 10 E, 20-300 m depth strata, total 2433 nmi2 =
 8 345 km2), biomass in tonnes (acoustic, trawl-allocated, TS gadoids 20 log L - 67.5, mackerel -77.2):
   herring 110 436; blue whiting 17 926; saithe 18 309; mackerel 16 219; horse mackerel 7 442;
   Norway pout 532; whiting 491; cod 360; haddock 15; sprat 2.
 Derived densities over 8 345 km2: blue whiting 2.1 t/km2 (mostly in the 60-300 m strata C4, C5, D4, D5:
 ~3.2 t/km2 there); mackerel 1.9; horse mackerel 0.9; Norway pout 0.06 t/km2. Single survey, August.
 Note the strata are 20-300 m; nothing deeper than 300 m surveyed. Mackerel TS -77.2 (older value; current
 -84.9 would raise mackerel numbers ~6 x for the same mackerel backscatter; derived).

2. Mackerel, IESSNS North Sea / Skagerrak extension (July) [F]
 Nottestad, L. et al. 2022. Cruise report from the International Ecosystem Summer Survey in the Nordic
 Seas (IESSNS) 1 July - 3 August 2022. WD to WGWIDE; Supplementary Document 4 to ICES 2023 WGIPS
 (ICES Sci. Rep. 5:74). https://ndownloader.figshare.com/files/41535324
 p. 7: "data collected with the IESSNS methodology from the Skagerrak and the northern and western part of
 the North Sea are now available for 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021 and 2022."
 Appendix 1, p. 53: Denmark (commercial vessel Ceton) since 2018; "In 2022, 34 stations ... extended into
 the eastern Skagerrak"; "Average mackerel catch in 2022 amounted 1689 kg/km2 ... (2021: 2429 kg/km2) ...
 (2020: 1318 kg/km2, 2019: 1009 kg/km2, 2018: 1743 kg/km2)"; "StoX ... estimate of mackerel biomass in
 the North Sea for 2022 is 471 948 tonnes ... The area of this polygon is 278 525 km2." [= 1.7 t/km2,
 derived]. Future stratum limits: "The northern limit for the North Sea and the Skagerrak were defined as
 60 N and 59 N ... The easter limit in the Skagerrak was set to 11 E" (i.e. the model box is inside the
 IESSNS North Sea stratum except east of 11 E). Swept-area surface trawl (0-~30 m), no acoustics in the
 North Sea part. Mackerel is a summer feeding visitor.

3. Norway pout [F] (stock level only)
 ICES 2023. Norway pout (Trisopterus esmarkii) in Subarea 4 and Division 3.a. ICES Advice, nop.27.3a4.
 https://ndownloader.figshare.com/files/42603106 : "SSB (4th quarter 2023) 78191 ... tonnes"; Blim = Bloss
 42 573 t. Stock assessed for the whole North Sea + 3.a, no Skagerrak split in the advice.
 Order of magnitude (derived, rough): 78 kt SSB spread over a ~150 000-300 000 km2 northern North Sea /
 Skagerrak distribution gives ~0.3-0.5 t/km2 SSB (total biomass with juveniles higher). The 1989 August
 acoustic survey gave only 0.06 t/km2 in the eastern Skagerrak strata (above). Dana bottom hauls in 44F8
 and 44G0 (2013) had Norway pout as a main catch species (Dana 2013 report, haul table). The caller's
 GOV estimate (Norway pout 16 % of 1.3 t/km2 = ~0.2 t/km2) is consistent with this order of magnitude.

4. Mesopelagic fish (Maurolicus muelleri, Benthosema glaciale) [A]
 Gjosaeter, J. 1986 (IMR Brage hdl 11250/115074; server unreachable, abstract via search snippet):
 acoustic surveys 1971-1976 at 38 and 50 kHz covering the Norwegian Deep, Skagerrak, the slope north of the
 North Sea and west of the British Isles. "In Norwegian waters Maurolicus muelleri was the dominant species
 and the estimated stock size in the area covered ranged between 20 000 and 1 600 000 tonnes"; "no seasonal
 variation in abundance could be traced"; "all the stock size estimates are tentative". Area not obtained,
 so no t/km2. [A]
 Gjosaeter & Kawaguchi 1980 (cited in Rasmussen & Giske 1994, Marine Biology; https://bio.uib.no/te/papers/Rasmussen_1994_Life-history.pdf):
 mesopelagic fish biomass "in these waters" (Norwegian fjords/coast) ~2.2 million t, "probably an
 underestimate" [S].
 Masfjorden (W Norway): pearlside dominates scattering layers shallower than ~200 m, B. glaciale below
 200-250 m [A, search snippet; bora.uib.no].
 Kaartvedt et al. 1993 (ICES C.M. 1993/L:44, https://www.ices.dk/sites/pub/CM%20Doccuments/1993/L/1993_L44.pdf):
 Norwegian shelf/slope 62-70 N, two deep scattering layers of pearlside/krill/lanternfish ~95 % of
 backscatter in the upper 500 m in winter [A; north of the Skagerrak].
 GAP: no Skagerrak/Norwegian Trench mesopelagic density in g/m2 found. Relevant for the box only in the
 deep (>150-200 m) Trench part; also excluded by the Danish HERAS 150 m rule in 2002-2013.
