HERAS methods relevant to converting acoustic estimates to t/km2 in the eastern Skagerrak
========================================================================================
Compiled 2026-10-24. All [F].

1. WHO SURVEYS THE SKAGERRAK/KATTEGAT
 - Denmark (DTU Aqua, RV Dana) since 1991: "Since 1991 the DTU National Institute of Aquatic
   Resources (DTU AQUA) has participated in the ICES co-ordinated herring acoustic survey ... with
   the responsibility for the surveying the Skagerrak and Kattegat area." (Dana 2013 report, Intro;
   same sentence in all Dana reports 2005-2021.) Danish area = east of 6 E, north of 56 N (strata
   21, 31, 41, 42, 151, 152).
 - Sweden, RV Argos: the PGHERS 1996 report (ICES C.M. 1996/H:3, figshare 19262786, file 1996_H3.pdf;
   scanned, OCR text partly garbled) has a national survey-history appendix listing Argos herring
   acoustic surveys with a one-boat Foto pelagic trawl in 1984 (21 Aug-1 Sep), 1985 (20 Aug-1 Sep),
   1986, 1988, 1989 (1-4 Aug) and 1990 (30 Jul-18 Aug), clupeid TS 20 log L - 71.2, gadoids -67.5, i.e.
   the Swedish Skagerrak/Kattegat summer acoustic survey of the 1980s. The same appendix lists Dana
   surveys of "Skagerrak and Kattegat" from 1984 onward. PGHERS 1996 main text: "The Skagerrak and
   Kattegat regions are also important areas for younger age groups and it is recommended that either
   Denmark or Sweden survey this area. Sweden is investigating the possibility of obtaining funds to
   deploy the research vessel Argos for such an exercise." -> From 1991 Denmark alone covers 3.a in
   HERAS. No Argos rectangle tables were found in PGHERS 1993-2008 or WGIPS 2010-2015 (Argos mentioned
   0-2 times per report, never as the 3.a HERAS vessel). Argos-era by-rectangle numbers (1984-1990)
   would be in the 1980s-1990 HAWG / "Report on the Herring Acoustic Surveys in the North Sea,
   Skagerrak and Kattegat" (e.g. summer 1989, IMR Brage hdl 11250/104797) - not retrieved (gap).
 - Survey window: Dana 22-28 June to 5-21 July (Dana reports 2013-2024; manual Table 5.1: 28 June-21 July 2022).

2. SPECIES ALLOCATION (CLU / MIX / HER / SPR)
 ICES 2025 IPS manual (TIMES 71), printed p. 81-82 (PDF p. 89-90), and HERAS 2023 report p. 4-5:
 "In strata 21, 31, 41, 42, 51, 61, 71, 131, 151, 152, covered by the German and Danish cruises,
 clupeids usually do not occur in single species schools but in mixed aggregations and schools that are
 comparatively clearly distinguishable from other schooling fishes. Post-processing of hydroacoustic data
 are therefore based on an aggregated CLU category consisting of a mix (in variable proportions) of
 clupeids herring (HER) and sprat (SPR) as well as in some occasions also anchovy (ANE) and sardines (PIL).
 Occasionally, spatially restricted occurrences of 'clean' schools of herring, sprat or sardine are
 observed. Then, species-specific categories are allocated ... In cases of mixed targeted catches of
 clupeids together with other species (horse mackerel, mackerel, various gadoids etc.) a combined MIX
 category is allocated to the respective echoes. ... All disaggregation steps of mixed acoustic categories
 to individual species in the German and Danish data are conducted using a Split-NASC process in the StoX
 software".
 Meaning: HER = pure herring; SPR = pure sprat; CLU = clupeid mix (HER, SPR, PIL, ANE) split by trawl
 catch composition; MIX = clupeids + non-clupeids (WHB, GUG, HAD, COD, GSE, HKB, NOP, POL, POK, MSE, WHG
 and others), split by trawl catch composition with each species' TS.
 HERAS 2023 p.15: "In the Danish acoustic data, the MIX category ..." [allocated in 2023]; recommendation:
 "mixed-species acoustic categories should only be used when there is no other [option]".
 Older Danish practice (Dana reports 2005-2019): total fish NASC split in proportion to trawl catch
 composition by subarea for herring, sprat, gadoids and mackerel ("The number of fish per species is
 assumed to be in proportion to the contribution of the given species in the trawl hauls").

3. TARGET STRENGTH (38 kHz, L = total length cm)
 Manual 2025 Table 5.8 (PDF p. 89):  HER, SPR, PIL, ANE: TS = 20 log L - 71.2 (ICES 1982)
   WHB 20 log L - 65.2; HOM, GUG, HAD, COD, HKB, NOP, POL, POK, WHG: 20 log L - 67.5;
   sandeels (GSE, MSE): 21.7 log L - 81.5; boarfish 20 log L - 66.2.
 Manual 2025 general TS list (printed ~p. 20): herring alternative 20 log L - 71.9 (Foote et al. 1987,
   used in IESNS/NSSH); mackerel 20 log L - 84.9 (Edwards et al. 1984); physoclists 20 log L - 67.5.
 Dana reports 2013-2020: herring and sprat -71.2; gadoids -67.5; mackerel -84.9.

4. UPPER (SURFACE) BLIND ZONE AND NEAR-BOTTOM EXCLUSION
 Dana: ES38-7 in towed body at 3-7 m (mostly 4-5 m); integration "from 3 m below the transducer to 1 m
   above the bottom or to max 500 m depth" (Dana 2019, 2020). Dana 2013 Table 1: "Integration start
   (absolute) depth 7-9 m"; "Bottom margin (backstep) 1.0 m". Manual 2025 Table 5.3 (PDF p. 79): Dana
   transducer depth 4-6 m, upper integration limit 8-10 m, max range 500 m. Other HERAS vessels: upper
   integration limits 10-12 m (Celtic Explorer 12, Johan Hjort 10, Scotia 12, Solea 10, Tridens 10).
 Pre-2010 Dana: integration to max 300 m (PGHERS 2006, survey 2005).
 150 m RULE (Dana 2002-2013 at least): "Historically, herring and sprat have not been observed in
   midwater trawl hauls at depths below 150 meters. Therefore, layers below 150 meter have been excluded
   from the estimation." (Dana 2013 PDF p.5; PGHERS 2002, 2003, 2004, 2005, 2006, 2007, 2008; WGIPS 2010,
   2012.) Not stated in the 2016-2020 reports, which say "to max 500 m". Relevance: the model box has mean
   depth 168 m and reaches 529 m; any planktivore biomass below 150 m (Norway pout, Maurolicus, blue
   whiting, deep herring in the Trench) was excluded from the Danish estimates in those years.
 Day/night: Dana samples both day and night; the manual notes that Kattegat/Skagerrak herring "is found
   in dispersed layers mixed with other fish at all times" (printed p. 64), so the night surface-rise
   problem is considered minor there.
 German (GERAS, western Baltic incl. SD21) manual ch. 7.8 (PDF p. 128): survey at night "due to a certain
   fraction of target species being distributed within the bottom dead zone during daytime (Bethke,
   1993)"; clupeids "anticipated to be present in the acoustic blind zone within the near-surface layers
   ... not quantified, since the integration start depth has been set to 10 m ... in some parts of the
   survey area, the blind zone/near field exclusion exceeds more than half of the total water column."
 SISP 9 (2015) p. 15-16: "The blind zone, which is the transducer draft + the nearfield zone may
   therefore be larger on some vessels than others." IESSNS caveat p. 22: "The acoustic dead zone at the
   surface (0-15 m depth)".
 HERAS 2023 p. 12: near-surface herring in the 2023 heatwave raised "concerns about some potential
   underestimation due to limited echosounder coverage issues (blind zones) near the surface and
   potential increased avoidance problems".
