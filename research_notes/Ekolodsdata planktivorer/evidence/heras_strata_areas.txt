DISCREPANCY: the 2016 Dana report gives ST41 = 1823 and ST42 = 3710 nmi2, the 2025 manual gives
41 = 3362 and 42 = 1784 nmi2 (labels effectively swapped; 21 and 31 also differ by ~6-10 %).
On the Dana 2019 strata map (Figure 1, see below) 41 is the large northern polygon along the
Norwegian coast (Norwegian Trench) and 42 the smaller southern polygon off Jutland, which fits the
manual (41 > 42). WGIPS 2018 (Table 5.14 footnote) also says 42 was "New strata in 2017, was part
of strata 41 in 2016", so the 2016 national geometry may not equal the current one.
Use the manual areas for 2017+ international estimates.

Figure 1 (PDF p. 14) "Map showing the survey area for the Danish acoustic survey with R/V Dana in
June-July 2019. The map shows the subareas (strata) used in the abundance estimation."
Section 2.2: "The survey was carried out in the Kattegat and Skagerrak area, east of 6 E and north of
56 N (Fig. 1). The area is split into 6 sub-areas ... spacing of 15 nm in strata 151, 17.5 nm in
strata 41 and 10 nm in strata 31 and 21."
Approximate geometry read off the map by this agent (coordinates +-0.05 deg, derived, not stated):
  - 41/31 boundary: straight line from ~10.0 E, 58.8 N (Norwegian coast) to ~10.6 E, 57.7 N (Skagen).
  - 41/42 boundary: from ~8.0 E, 57.5 N to ~10.4 E, 58.05 N.
  - 31/21 boundary: from Skagen (~10.6 E, 57.7 N) to ~11.4 E, 57.8 N (Swedish coast).
  - 21 = Kattegat; 31 = eastern Skagerrak (Swedish side, east of the Skagen-Arendal line);
    41 = northern Skagerrak / Norwegian Trench; 42 = southern Skagerrak off Jutland;
    151/152 = eastern North Sea (6-8 E).
Overlap with the model box (57.7-58.4 N, 9.9-11.3 E), derived estimate: ~60-65 % of the box is in
stratum 31, ~20 % in the eastern tip of 41 (north of ~58.05 N, west of ~10.2-10.4 E), ~10-15 % in
42 (south-west corner), and a sliver of 21 in the south-east corner (south of ~57.8 N, east of 10.6 E).
