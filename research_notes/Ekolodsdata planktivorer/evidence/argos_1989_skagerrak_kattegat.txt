Section "Report on the 1989 acoustic survey by R/V Argos in Skagerrak and Kattegat" (O. Hagstrom and
L-E. Palmen, IMR Lysekil), PDF p. 7:
 "As part of the ICES-coordinated acoustic survey of the North Sea and Division IIIa R/V 'Argos'
 surveyed the western part of Skagerrak and Kattegat from 1-4 August 1989." [the strata used, C and D,
 are the Danish strata EAST of 10 E, so "western" probably means the Swedish/eastern Skagerrak - the
 agent's reading; the C/D definitions are in heras_strata_areas.txt]
 "The methods of stratification used are the same as used in the Danish survey. A total of 12 pelagic
 hauls were made". TS: herring, sprat and horse mackerel 20 log L - 71.2; mackerel and dogfish
 20 log L - 77.2; gadoids 20 log L - 67.5. EK400 38 kHz.
 "Based on vertebral counts all herring of 2 group and older could be assigned to local spring spawner
 stock. North Sea autumn spawners dominated the 0- and 1-group."
Danish stratification (Dana section, PDF p. 6): "The surveyed area is stratified in subareas (1 deg
 lat - 2 deg long). Subareas with large variation in depth is divided in up to six strata. 1. 0-20 m;
 2. 20-40 m; 3. 40-60 m; 4. 60-100 m; 5. 100-300 m; 6. > 300 m."
 -> INFERRED by this agent (not stated in the Argos section): Argos strata C3 = subarea C, 40-60 m depth,
    D4 = subarea D, 60-100 m, etc. Caveat: the Kattegat strata are E0-E3, and "0" is not in the 1-6 depth
    scheme, so the suffix may not be a depth code everywhere. The depth labels in the table below are inferred.

Relevance (depends on the inferred depth labels above): early August 1989, herring biomass in the eastern Skagerrak was concentrated in the 20-60 m
depth strata along the coast (34-43 t/km2), with low densities over 60-300 m (0.7-6 t/km2). A box with
mean depth 168 m would, by these strata, be closer to the 100-300 m values. Single year, August.
