# Evidence C - Benthivore score of the minor gadoids: morphology first, diet as a check

Scope: one 0-1 benthivore score per species for the pooled gadoid group (phi), on the scale of
`../Bentos åtkomlighet för torskfiskar/benthos_accessibility.md` section 3 (haddock 1.0, cod
0.5-1.0, whiting 0-0.3, saithe/hake 0, Norway pout 0-0.2). The score comes from **morphology
and habitat only** (mouth position, jaw relation, barbels, demersal vs pelagic life, feeding
habitat). Diet by mass is a check and never moves a score.
Tags: [F] full text or full page read; [A] abstract or abstract snippet only; [S] secondary
(the encoding source is named, the original paper was not read). FishBase diet records are
**[S]**: they were encoded by FishBase from papers not read here.
Box: Norwegian Trench slope, Skagerrak, ~58 N 10-11 E, 44-529 m, mostly 100-300 m mud.

"Strict" benthos = benthic invertebrates (polychaetes, bivalves, echinoderms, Nephrops, Calocaris,
Crangon, Processa, Pontophilus, Munida/galatheids, crabs, pagurids, gammarid amphipods,
isopods). Pasiphaea and Pandalus/Pandalidae are reported separately (borderline). Hyperiids
(*Parathemisto*), *Paraeuchaeta*, euphausiids, mysids and cephalopods are counted pelagic. The
reassignment from FishBase's categories is **mine** (section 3). Rabbitfish (*Chimaera*) is not a
gadiform and is left out.

**Score rationale** (same rules as the earlier note: inferior mouth or lower jaw shorter +
barbel + bottom-bound = high; lower jaw longer, superior mouth, no barbel, pelagic schooling =
0):
- *Cod band (0.5-1.0)*: poor cod, bib and tusk have the cod configuration (terminal mouth,
  lower jaw shorter, chin barbel). Tusk is more bottom-bound than cod (solitary, does not school
  off the bottom), so its central value is higher (0.75). Poor cod and bib form small schools
  above the bottom, so 0.7.
- *Haddock end (0.7-1.0)*: rocklings (3-7 barbels, "bottom-living", sedentary) and tadpole fish
  (lower jaw shorter, barbel, depressed head). *G. macrophthalmus* is lowered to 0.5-1.0 for its
  canine teeth (a piscivore trait).
- *Forkbeard 0.4-0.9*: terminal mouth and barbel as cod, long pelvic rays near the bottom,
  but benthopelagic. FAO gives no jaw relation. Whether the pelvic rays are chemosensory was not
  checked. That is the reason for the wide range.
- *Ling 0.2-0.6*: a large barbel (benthic sensing) but subequal jaws, "strongly developed teeth"
  and rocky habitat. This sits between whiting (0-0.3) and cod (0.5-1.0).
- *0-0.2*: blue ling, pollack, blue whiting (lower jaw longer, no or small barbel), Norway pout
  and silvery pout (superior/oblique mouth, pelagic schooling).
- *Grenadier 0.1-0.5*: the inferior mouth points to the bottom, but the barbel is minute and the
  habit is bentho-/bathypelagic. Most of its range lies below the box.

## 3. Notes on the reclassification and the data

- FishBase's "zoobenthos" category does not match the definition used here. In the Norwegian
  Deep records it contains *Parathemisto abyssorum* (pelagic hyperiid), *Paraeuchaeta
  norvegica* (pelagic copepod), *Pasiphaea* spp. and *Pandalus borealis*. For the grenadier it
  also contains cephalopods. Taking these out drops Norway pout from FishBase's 2-23 % to
  ~0-1 %, and pollack from 35 % to ~6 %.
- Tusk's 78 % fish in the Norwegian Deep rests on **4 stomachs** and should not be read as the
  diet of the species. NW Atlantic tusk and the FAO text point to a mainly benthic-crustacean
  diet.
- The Sorbe values are **% volume** of 10-35 cm fish. Polychaetes are Cv 11.4 % in Table 2; the text says "Cv = 28,3 %" (the Natantia figure). The table value is used because only it makes the column sum to ~100 %. The fish share rises with size ("doit
  devenir prépondérante dans l'alimentation des individus les plus âgés du talus").
- In the FishBase *G. thori* record the displayed percentages are rescaled from the paper's %W:
  "54.2 ... %W=52". Use the %W values. The same rescaling appears in the grenadier records.
- Silvery pout taxonomy: the Norwegian Deep and Skagerrak form is *Gadiculus thori* (Albert 1993:
  "*G. argenteus thori*"). The *G. argenteus* diet records on FishBase are Mediterranean
  (Balearic) and are not used.
- Bergstad 1991 Part 1 covers saithe, cod, whiting, blue whiting, pollack, ling, blue ling,
  tusk and hake (search-engine abstract summary of
  https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/00364827.1991.10413455 [A]). FishBase encoded only
  tusk, blue ling and pollack. Ling and blue whiting values from the Norwegian Deep exist in the
  paper but were not reachable (HTTP 403).
